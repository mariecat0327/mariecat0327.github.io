概述 · 一眼识技艺

肇兴侗寨是侗族传统手工艺的"活态博物馆"。这里的蓝靛染、蜡染、刺绣、银饰锻造等非遗技艺， 至今仍在传承人的手中代代相传，延续着千年的民族记忆。

侗族刺绣以其独特的100+种针法闻名，图案蕴含着丰富的民族图腾与自然意象。蓝靛染和蜡染 则运用植物染料和蜂蜡防染工艺，在布料上创造出深邃的蓝色与灵动的冰纹肌理，每一件作品都独一无二。

寨内的少数民族服饰私人珍藏博物馆收藏了大量侗、苗等民族的传统服饰与手工艺品， 是了解西南少数民族服饰文化的绝佳窗口。游客还可参与手作体验课程，在传承人的指导下 亲手制作一件属于自己的非遗作品。

蓝靛染 · 草木之灵

侗族蓝靛染采用天然植物板蓝根为染料，经过浸泡、发酵、打靛等多道工序， 染出深邃而温润的蓝色。蓝靛染的布料耐洗耐磨，越洗越蓝，是侗族人世代相传的智慧。 在肇兴，几乎每家每户都保留着染布的传统。

蜡染 · 冰纹之美

蜡染是侗族最具特色的印染技艺之一。匠人用蜡刀蘸取熔化的蜂蜡，在布面上绘制精美图案， 再浸入蓝靛染缸中染色。蜡层自然龟裂形成的"冰纹"，使每一件蜡染作品都独一无二。 图案多为花鸟鱼虫、几何纹样，承载着侗族的图腾信仰与审美追求。

刺绣 · 指尖上的史诗

侗族刺绣以"百种针法、千般图案"著称。绣娘们用五彩丝线在布面上绣出日月星辰、 花鸟走兽、神话传说。侗绣不仅是服饰的装饰，更是一部穿在身上的"史诗"—— 记录着侗族的迁徙历史、神话传说与日常生活。每一幅绣品，都是一段无声的故事。

珍藏博物馆 · 服饰宝库

寨内的少数民族服饰私人珍藏博物馆，收藏了侗、苗、水、瑶等民族的 传统服饰与手工艺品。从精美的银饰头冠到繁复的刺绣衣裙， 每一件藏品都诉说着西南少数民族的审美智慧与文化记忆， 是了解民族服饰文化的宝贵窗口。

Overview · At a Glance

Zhaoxing Dong Village is a "living museum" of traditional Dong handicrafts. Crafts such as indigo dyeing, batik, embroidery, and silver forging are still passed down through generations, preserving millennia of ethnic memory.

Dong embroidery is renowned for its 100+ unique stitching techniques, with patterns rich in ethnic totems and natural imagery. Indigo dyeing and batik use natural plant dyes and beeswax resist techniques to create deep blues and mesmerizing crackled textures — each piece one of a kind.

The village's Private Ethnic Costume Museum houses a vast collection of traditional Dong, Miao, and other minority costumes and handicrafts. Visitors can also join hands-on workshops to create their own heritage craft under the guidance of master artisans.

Culture & Stories · A Thousand-Year Legacy

Indigo Dye · Spirit of Plants

Dong indigo dyeing uses natural plant dyes from the indigo plant. Through soaking, fermenting, and vatting, it produces a deep, warm blue that becomes richer with each wash. This ancient wisdom is still preserved in almost every household in Zhaoxing.

Batik · Cracked Ice Beauty

Batik is one of the Dong's most distinctive crafts. Artisans draw patterns with hot beeswax, then dip the cloth into indigo dye. The natural cracks in the wax create unique "ice patterns" — each piece is one of a kind, carrying Dong totemic beliefs and aesthetic values.

Embroidery · Epic on Fingertips

Dong embroidery is famous for its "hundreds of stitches and thousands of patterns." Using colorful silk threads, embroiderers create sun, moon, flowers, birds, and mythical tales. Dong embroidery is more than decoration — it is a "woven epic" recording the Dong people's migration history and daily life.

Costume Museum · A Treasure Trove

The village's Private Ethnic Costume Museum houses a rich collection of traditional costumes and handicrafts from the Dong, Miao, Shui, and Yao peoples. From elaborate silver headpieces to intricately embroidered garments, each piece tells a story of cultural memory and aesthetic wisdom.
