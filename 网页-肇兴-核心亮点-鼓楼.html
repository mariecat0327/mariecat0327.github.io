鼓楼 DRUM TOWER

侗族村寨的灵魂 · 榫卯结构的奇迹
概述 · 一眼识鼓楼

鼓楼是侗族村寨的标志性建筑，被誉为侗族文化的"灵魂"和"心脏"。肇兴侗寨拥有五座鼓楼，分别以"仁、义、礼、智、信"命名，集中体现了侗族人民对儒家伦理的尊崇与内化。

每座鼓楼均为全木结构，采用古老的榫卯技艺，不用一钉一铆，却坚固屹立数百年。其中最高的信团鼓楼达24.37米，其精湛的建筑工艺被载入吉尼斯世界纪录。

鼓楼不仅是建筑艺术的瑰宝，更是侗族社会生活的中心——议事、节庆、娱乐、迎宾都在这里举行。它是侗族人精神世界的象征，也是中华传统建筑智慧的活态传承。

鼓楼 · 侗族的精神地标

在侗族文化中，鼓楼是村寨的"心脏"。它不仅是建筑，更是侗族人集体记忆的容器。 每逢节庆、议事或重大活动，族人便聚集于鼓楼之下，击鼓传讯、共商族务。 鼓楼的存在，维系着侗族社会的团结与秩序。

榫卯技艺 · 不用一钉的千年智慧

肇兴鼓楼完全依靠榫卯结构连接，木构件之间通过精确的凹凸咬合，不借助任何金属构件。 这种技艺不仅体现了侗族工匠对木材特性的深刻理解，更展现了"道法自然"的东方哲学。 数百年风雨侵蚀，鼓楼依然屹立，堪称建筑奇迹。

仁义礼智信 · 五座鼓楼的文化密码

五座鼓楼以"仁、义、礼、智、信"命名，对应五个家族（团）。 这种命名方式将儒家核心伦理与侗族社会结构融为一体， 体现了侗族文化对中华主流价值观的吸收与创新。 每一座鼓楼都是一个家族的精神图腾。

吉尼斯世界纪录 · 世界的认可

肇兴鼓楼群以其独特的建筑技艺和恢宏气势，被载入吉尼斯世界纪录， 成为世界瞩目的侗族文化瑰宝。每年吸引数十万中外游客前来探访， 成为贵州乃至中国最具代表性的民族文化地标之一。

Overview · At a Glance

The Drum Tower is the iconic landmark of the Dong village, known as the "soul" and "heart" of Dong culture. Zhaoxing has five Drum Towers, named after Ren, Yi, Li, Zhi, Xin (Benevolence, Righteousness, Propriety, Wisdom, Faithfulness), reflecting the Dong people's deep respect for Confucian ethics.

Each tower is entirely timber-framed using ancient mortise-and-tenon joinery — without a single nail or rivet. The tallest, Xin Tower, stands at 24.37 meters and its craftsmanship has been recognized by the Guinness World Records.

The Drum Tower is not only an architectural treasure but also the center of Dong social life — where councils, festivals, entertainment, and welcoming ceremonies take place. It symbolizes the spiritual world of the Dong people and is a living testament to China's traditional architectural wisdom.

Culture & Stories · A Thousand-Year Legacy

The Drum Tower · Spiritual Landmark

In Dong culture, the Drum Tower is the "heart" of the village. It is not just a building but a vessel of collective memory. During festivals, councils, or major events, the community gathers beneath the tower to beat drums and discuss tribal affairs. The Drum Tower sustains the unity and order of Dong society.

Joinery · A Thousand-Year Wisdom Without Nails

The Zhaoxing Drum Towers rely entirely on mortise-and-tenon joinery. Wooden components interlock precisely without any metal fasteners. This craft demonstrates the Dong artisans' profound understanding of wood and embodies the philosophy of "harmony with nature." Standing strong for centuries through rain and wind, they remain an architectural marvel.

Ren, Yi, Li, Zhi, Xin · A Cultural Code

The five towers are named Ren, Yi, Li, Zhi, and Xin, corresponding to five clans (Tuan). This naming integrates core Confucian ethics into Dong social structure, reflecting the Dong people's absorption and innovation of mainstream Chinese values. Each tower is the spiritual emblem of a clan.

Guinness World Record · Global Recognition

The Zhaoxing Drum Towers have been inscribed in the Guinness World Records for their unique craftsmanship and grandeur. They attract hundreds of thousands of visitors annually, standing as one of Guizhou's — and even China's — most iconic ethnic cultural landmarks.
