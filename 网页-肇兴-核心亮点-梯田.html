概述 · 一眼识梯田

肇兴侗寨周边的堂安梯田与夏格梯田，是黔东南最具代表性的梯田景观。 其中，堂安梯田被《中国国家地理》评为“中国最美徒步路线”之一， 层层叠叠的梯田从山腰铺展至谷底，四季变幻如画。

堂安侗寨是中国与挪威合作建立的全国唯一一座侗族生态博物馆， 整个寨子就是一座“活着的博物馆”。而藏在半山腰的夏格梯田， 则更显原始与野趣，游客稀少，可以独享整片梯田的宁静。

两片梯田相距不远，由一条青石板古道串联， 沿途可欣赏梯田、溪流、古树和原生态侗寨， 是徒步爱好者的天堂。

堂安梯田

Tang'an Terraces · 云上梯田

堂安梯田位于肇兴侗寨东北侧约6公里处， 海拔约840米。梯田从山腰层层叠叠铺展至谷底， 视野极为开阔。清晨晨雾缭绕时，梯田与侗寨交织，美如仙境。 堂安侗寨三面环山，一面是空旷的梯田，是中国与挪威合作建立的 全国唯一一座侗族生态博物馆，整个村寨就是一座活态的博物馆。

夏格梯田

Xiage Terraces · 半山秘境

夏格梯田藏在肇兴侗寨与堂安梯田之间的半山腰上，海拔约710米。 与堂安相比，夏格更显原始与野趣，游客稀少，可以独享整片梯田的宁静。 夏格侗寨被列入第二批中国传统村落名录， 村内拥有6座鼓楼、5座戏台、2座风雨桥。梯田依山就势开垦， 曲线优美如“被山风揉过的绸缎”。

Overview · At a Glance

The Tang'an Terraces and Xiage Terraces around Zhaoxing Dong Village are among the most iconic terrace landscapes in southeastern Guizhou. Tang'an Terraces have been rated by Chinese National Geography as one of "China's Most Beautiful Hiking Routes", with layers of terraced fields cascading from the mountainside to the valley, changing beautifully with the seasons.

Tang'an Dong Village is the only Dong Eco-museum in China, established in cooperation with Norway — the entire village is a "living museum". Meanwhile, the Xiage Terraces, hidden halfway up the mountain, offer a more pristine and rustic experience with fewer visitors, allowing you to enjoy the tranquility of the terraces all to yourself.

The two terraces are not far apart, connected by an ancient cobblestone path. Along the way, you can enjoy terraces, streams, ancient trees, and authentic Dong villages — a paradise for hiking enthusiasts.

Tang'an Terraces

Tang'an · Above the Clouds

Located about 6 km northeast of Zhaoxing, at an elevation of about 840 meters. The terraces spread layer upon layer from the mountainside to the valley, offering a vast panorama. In the early morning mist, the terraces and the Dong village blend into a fairy-tale scene. Tang'an Dong Village is surrounded by mountains on three sides and opens to the terraces on the fourth — it is the only Dong Eco-museum in China, a living museum in itself.

Xiage Terraces

Xiage · Hidden Halfway

Xiage Terraces are nestled halfway between Zhaoxing and Tang'an, at an elevation of about 710 meters. Compared to Tang'an, Xiage is more pristine and rustic, with far fewer visitors, allowing you to enjoy the serenity of the terraces all to yourself. Xiage Dong Village is listed in China's Second Batch of Traditional Villages, with 6 drum towers, 5 stages, and 2 wind-rain bridges. The terraces follow the contours of the mountain, their curves as graceful as "silk ruffled by mountain breezes."
