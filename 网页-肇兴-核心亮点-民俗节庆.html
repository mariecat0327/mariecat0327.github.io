A Thousand-Year Dong Village · Living Culture
Zhaoxing is one of the largest Dong villages in China, named by "Chinese National Geography" as one of the "Six Most Beautiful Ancient Villages in China". Over 2,200 years of Dong history is passed down here. The Dong Grand Song echoes through the valleys, while drum towers and flower bridges have witnessed centuries of wind and rain.

Practical Info · Before You Go

Transport

  • High-speed rail: Congjiang Station
  • Shuttle: Pick-up service from station to village
  • Duration: 15-20 min
  • Direct via Guiyang-Guangzhou line

Best Time to Visit

  • Peak Apr–Oct: 15–30°C, light jacket, T-shirt, sunscreen, rain gear for showers.
  • Off-peak Nov–Mar: 0–15°C, damp and chilly, pack down jacket, thermal underwear, waterproof shoes.
  • Rainy season concentrates in May–July, bring an umbrella.

Accommodation

  • Stilt-house B&Bs — scenic views
  • Creek-side inns — peaceful sounds
  • Terrace-view lodges — sunrise vistas
  • Boutique hotels available inside the village

Travel Tips

  • Entry fee: 80 RMB/person
  • Sightseeing shuttle: 20 RMB round-trip
  • Languages: Mandarin / Dong
  • Suggested stay: 2-4 days
