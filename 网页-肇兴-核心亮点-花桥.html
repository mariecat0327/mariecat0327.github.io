花桥 FLOWER BRIDGE

侗族建筑三宝之一 · 风雨廊桥的千年守望
概述 · 一眼识花桥

花桥，又称风雨桥、廊桥，是侗族建筑"三宝"之一（鼓楼、花桥、寨门）。肇兴侗寨的花桥横跨溪流之上， 既是交通要道，也是村民休憩、聚会、避雨纳凉的重要公共空间。

花桥为全木结构，采用古老的榫卯技艺建造，桥身建有廊亭，可遮风挡雨。桥廊两侧设有长凳， 供行人歇脚。桥顶飞檐翘角，造型优美，与鼓楼共同构成侗寨最动人的天际线。

花桥不仅是实用的桥梁建筑，更是侗族人民精神生活的延伸——青年男女在此对歌、老人在此闲话家常、 孩童在此嬉戏玩耍。它是侗寨中最具生活气息的场所之一。

文化背景与历史故事 · 千年传承

花桥 · 侗族建筑的瑰宝

花桥与鼓楼、寨门并称"侗族建筑三宝"。它集桥、廊、亭于一体， 是侗族工匠智慧的集中体现。桥身横跨溪流，廊亭遮蔽风雨， 既满足了交通功能，又成为村民社交生活的中心场所。

风雨廊桥 · 侗乡的爱情故事

在侗族传统中，花桥是青年男女对歌传情的重要场所。 月光明媚的夜晚，姑娘小伙在桥廊中唱起情歌， 歌声随着溪水飘荡，花桥见证了无数侗家儿女的爱情故事。

榫卯智慧 · 木构的千年传奇

花桥与鼓楼一样，全部采用榫卯结构连接，不用一钉一铆。 木构件之间的精确咬合，使桥梁在风雨中屹立百年。 这种古老的建筑智慧，至今仍令现代建筑学家叹为观止。

花桥之下 · 侗寨的烟火日常

花桥是侗寨最具生活气息的地方。清晨，老人在此闲坐聊天； 午后，孩童在桥廊中嬉戏；傍晚，劳作归来的村民在桥上歇脚。 花桥承载着侗族人最平凡的日常，也凝聚着最深厚的情感。

Overview · At a Glance

The Flower Bridge, also known as the Wind-Rain Bridge or Covered Bridge, is one of the "Three Treasures" of Dong architecture (along with the Drum Tower and the Village Gate). Spanning across the streams of Zhaoxing Dong Village, it serves not only as a thoroughfare but also as a vital public space for villagers to rest, gather, and shelter from the weather.

Built entirely of timber using ancient mortise-and-tenon joinery, the bridge features a covered gallery with eaves that provide shade and shelter. Benches line both sides for travelers to rest. The upturned eaves and elegant silhouette create a stunning skyline alongside the Drum Tower.

More than just a practical structure, the Flower Bridge is deeply woven into Dong spiritual life — young lovers sing to each other here, elders chat, and children play. It is one of the most vibrant and living spaces in the village.

Culture & Stories · A Thousand-Year Legacy

A Treasure of Dong Architecture

The Flower Bridge, together with the Drum Tower and the Village Gate, forms the "Three Treasures" of Dong architecture. Combining bridge, corridor, and pavilion, it embodies the wisdom of Dong craftsmen — spanning streams while providing shelter and social space.

A Stage for Love Stories

In Dong tradition, the Flower Bridge is where young men and women sing love songs to each other. On moonlit nights, their voices echo across the water, and the bridge becomes a silent witness to countless romances.

Ancient Joinery Wisdom

Like the Drum Tower, the Flower Bridge is built entirely with mortise-and-tenon joinery — without a single nail or rivet. The precision of these interlocking wooden components has allowed the bridge to stand for over a century, a marvel admired by architects worldwide.

Daily Life Under the Bridge

The Flower Bridge is the heart of village life. Here, elders chat in the morning, children play in the afternoon, and farmers rest after work. It is where the most ordinary yet profound moments of Dong life unfold.
