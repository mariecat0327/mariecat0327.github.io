食在肇兴 · 侗乡风味

肇兴侗寨的美食，是酸、辣、鲜、香的完美融合， 也是侗族人民千年生活的智慧结晶。从酸汤火锅的醇厚、 脆皮烤香猪肉的焦香，到腌鱼腌肉的岁月沉淀， 每一道美食都承载着侗族人对自然的理解与对生活的热爱。

在肇兴，美食不仅满足味蕾，更是一种文化体验。 走进街巷，随处可见晾晒的腌鱼、飘香的酸汤、热气腾腾的糯米饭。 这里的每一道菜，都有它的故事与灵魂。

美食文化 · 舌尖上的侗乡

酸 · 侗族饮食的灵魂

侗族人常说"三天不吃酸，走路打捞蹿"。酸汤、酸鱼、酸肉—— 酸味是侗族饮食的底色。在缺盐少油的年代，侗族先民 用发酵的方式保存食物，创造了独特的酸食文化。 一碗酸汤，承载着侗族人的生存智慧与生活哲学。

发酵 · 时间的味道

腌鱼腌肉、酸汤——侗族美食的精髓在于"等待"。 将鲜鱼鲜肉封入木桶，让时间与微生物共同作用， 数月之后，开桶的瞬间香气四溢。这种与时间合作的 烹饪哲学，是侗族人对自然最深的敬畏。

大山 · 食材的馈赠

肇兴的美食离不开黔东南大山的馈赠。野生木姜子、 山泉水、稻田鱼、黑毛猪、高山茶——每一味食材 都来自这片土地。侗族人懂得如何让山野的味道 在餐桌上绽放，这是人与自然的默契。

传承 · 非遗美食的活态

酸汤鱼的制作技艺、腌鱼的发酵工艺，都已被列入 非物质文化遗产名录。在肇兴，美食不是旅游的附庸， 而是活着的传统——走进任何一户人家，你都能 品尝到千年不变的味道。

Taste of Zhaoxing · Dong Flavors

The cuisine of Zhaoxing Dong Village is a perfect blend of sour, spicy, fresh, and fragrant — a thousand-year testament to the wisdom of the Dong people. From the rich Sour Soup Hotpot to the crispy Roast Pork and the time-aged Fermented Fish & Meat, every dish embodies the Dong people's understanding of nature and their love for life.

In Zhaoxing, food is not just about taste — it's a cultural experience. Walk through the alleys and you'll see fermented fish drying in the sun, fragrant sour soup steaming, and sticky rice freshly cooked. Every dish has its story and soul.

Food Culture · Flavors of the Dong Highlands

Sour — The Soul of Dong Cuisine

The Dong people say, "Three days without sour, and you can barely walk." Sour soup, sour fish, sour meat — sourness is the foundation of Dong food. In times when salt was scarce, the Dong people used fermentation to preserve food, creating a unique sour-food culture that embodies their survival wisdom.

Fermentation — The Taste of Time

Fermented fish, fermented meat, sour soup — the essence of Dong cuisine is "waiting." Fresh ingredients are sealed in wooden barrels, allowing time and microbes to work. Months later, the aroma that bursts forth is unforgettable — a culinary philosophy that reflects the Dong people's deepest respect for nature.

Mountains — Nature's Bounty

Zhaoxing's cuisine is inseparable from the gifts of the southeastern Guizhou mountains. Wild pepper, spring water, rice-field fish, black-haired pigs, highland tea — every ingredient comes from this land. The Dong people know how to let the flavors of the mountains shine at the table — a harmony between humans and nature.

Living Heritage — The Art of Intangible Culture

The craft of sour fish soup and the fermentation of fish have been inscribed as intangible cultural heritage. In Zhaoxing, food is not a tourist accessory but a living tradition — walk into any household, and you can taste flavors that have remained unchanged for a thousand years.
