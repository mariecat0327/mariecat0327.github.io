侗族大歌 DONG GRAND SONG

人类非物质文化遗产 · 天籁之音穿越千年
概述 · 一眼识侗歌

侗族大歌是侗族民间多声部合唱音乐的总称，起源于春秋战国时期， 已有2500多年的历史。2009年，侗族大歌被列入联合国教科文组织人类非物质文化遗产代表作名录。

侗族大歌以无伴奏、多声部为最大特色，模仿鸟叫虫鸣、高山流水等自然之声， 形成了独特的音乐语汇。歌队由同村寨的男女组成，分高声部和低声部， 歌声和谐优美，被誉为"天籁之音"。

侗族大歌不仅是音乐艺术，更是侗族文化的百科全书——它承载着侗族的历史、传说、 伦理与情感，是侗族人精神生活的重要组成部分。在肇兴侗寨，至今仍有近百支村歌队 活跃在寨子中，传承着这份千年不绝的文化遗产。

文化背景与历史故事 · 千年传承

人类非遗 · 世界的声音

2009年，侗族大歌被联合国教科文组织列入"人类非物质文化遗产代表作名录"， 成为全人类共同的文化瑰宝。这一荣誉不仅是对侗族音乐艺术的最高认可， 也让世界听到了来自中国深山的天籁之音。

多声部奇迹 · 自然的模仿

侗族大歌的独特之处在于其无伴奏多声部合唱形式——歌者不依赖任何乐器， 仅凭人声便创造出和谐的和声效果。歌声模仿鸟鸣、流水、风声， 仿佛将整个大自然的声音都融入其中，被誉为"东方无伴奏交响乐"。

歌队传承 · 活态的文化

在肇兴侗寨，唱歌是每个侗族人生活的一部分。从孩童时代起， 孩子们便在歌师的教导下学习大歌。男女歌队在鼓楼、花桥、田间对歌， 歌声伴随着侗族人的一生——从出生到婚嫁，从劳作到节庆。

大歌里的故事 · 侗族的情感密码

侗族大歌不仅是音乐，更是一部"有声的史诗"。它唱颂祖先的迁徙、 讲述英雄的故事、表达青年男女的爱慕、传递农耕的智慧。 每一首大歌都承载着侗族人的情感与记忆，是侗族文化最鲜活的载体。

Overview · At a Glance

The Dong Grand Song is a general term for the Dong ethnic group's multi-part choral music, originating from the Spring and Autumn period over 2,500 years ago. In 2009, it was inscribed on the UNESCO Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity.

The Dong Grand Song is characterized by its unaccompanied multi-part harmony, imitating natural sounds like birdsong, insects, and flowing water. Singing groups, composed of men and women from the same village, perform with high and low vocal parts, creating harmonies so beautiful they are called "heavenly sounds."

More than just music, the Dong Grand Song is an encyclopedia of Dong culture — carrying history, legends, ethics, and emotions. In Zhaoxing, nearly 100 village singing groups keep this thousand-year heritage alive.

Culture & Stories · A Thousand-Year Legacy

UNESCO Heritage · A Voice of the World

In 2009, the Dong Grand Song was inscribed on the UNESCO Representative List, becoming a cultural treasure shared by all humanity. This honor not only recognizes the artistic excellence of Dong music but also brings the heavenly voices from China's deep mountains to the world.

The Miracle of Polyphony · Imitating Nature

The Dong Grand Song's uniqueness lies in its unaccompanied multi-part harmony. Without any instruments, singers create rich harmonies that imitate birds, flowing water, and wind — as if the entire natural world is woven into the music.

Living Heritage · Passing It On

In Zhaoxing, singing is part of everyday life. Children learn Grand Songs from master singers at a young age. Men and women sing in duels at drum towers and flower bridges — the songs accompany them through birth, marriage, work, and festivals.

Stories in the Songs · The Dong Emotional Code

The Dong Grand Song is more than music — it is a "living epic." It tells stories of ancestral migrations, heroic legends, young love, and farming wisdom. Each song carries the emotions and memories of the Dong people, making it the most vibrant vessel of their culture.
